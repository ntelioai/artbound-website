The surface

A private auction, held in a message thread.

The auction surface is the thread your collectors already read. ArtBound runs the room: opens the lot, holds the reserve, keeps time across regions, records the close.

The gallery writes in its own voice. Collectors bid in the language and channel they prefer. The AB agent handles the mechanics — pacing, extensions, confirmations, the record — in the background.

A collector is invited, RSVPs in one tap and is given a paddle, not a profile. Bids are confirmed before they land. Self-bidding is refused. A late bid extends the close. The hammer is recorded and handed to the gallery.

The session at the top of this page is that pipeline, played out message by message — no catalogue, no microsite, no public page. The thread is the venue.

The stance

Not a channel. Not a feed. Not a marketplace.

What ArtBound is not

  • A consumer marketplace.
  • A discovery platform or feed.
  • A third-party channel that disintermediates the gallery.
  • A replacement for the gallery’s relationship with its collectors.
  • A public catalogue or a storefront.
  • A broker, a payment provider, or a party to the sale.

What ArtBound is

  • Auction infrastructure, licensed to galleries.
  • Private, invitation-only, conversational.
  • AI-native, WhatsApp-first, multi-region.
  • Orchestration only — the session, and nothing beyond it.
  • Almost invisible — and that is the point.
