03How bidding works

Auctions run as a conversation — in a WhatsApp thread with the ArtBound agent, an automated assistant operating on the gallery’s behalf. The mechanics for a given sale are stated in the invitation and in the thread, and typically work as follows.

3.1 Lots, estimates, and reserves

Estimates are the gallery’s opinion of likely value, expressed before premium and taxes. They are not predictions, valuations, or guarantees. A lot may carry a confidential reserve — a minimum below which it will not be sold. Reserves are not disclosed and are never above the high estimate.

3.2 Placing a bid — the confirmation step

A message expressing interest, naming a number, or asking a question is not a bid. A bid is placed only when the agent puts a specific amount to you and you confirm it in the thread as instructed. If you do not confirm, no bid exists.

ONCE CONFIRMED, A BID IS A BINDING, IRREVOCABLE OFFER TO BUY THE LOT AT THAT AMOUNT, PLUS BUYER’S PREMIUM AND ANY APPLICABLE TAXES, DUTIES, AND SHIPPING. IT CANNOT BE WITHDRAWN, REDUCED, OR MADE CONDITIONAL.

3.3 Increments, outbidding, and extensions

  • Bids must meet or exceed the increment the agent states. The gallery may vary increments during a sale.
  • You cannot outbid yourself. If you already hold the leading bid, the agent will decline a further bid from you until someone else bids.
  • You will be told when you are leading and when you have been outbid. You will not be told who else is bidding (see 08).
  • Where anti-sniping applies, a bid confirmed in the closing minutes extends the lot’s close by the stated interval, repeating until the interval passes with no new bid.

3.4 Close, hammer, and the gallery’s discretion

A lot is sold when the gallery accepts the leading bid — the hammer. Until then, the gallery may, at its sole discretion and without liability: withdraw, split, or combine lots; refuse or cancel any bid; reopen bidding on a lot where a bid was missed, disputed, or made in error; re-offer an unsold lot; or cancel a sale. An unsold lot is one that did not reach its reserve.

3.5 Fixed-price offers

Some lots are offered at a fixed price rather than by auction. Confirming a purchase on such a lot is likewise a binding offer, accepted when the gallery confirms; the same fees, payment, and delivery terms apply.

04Messaging, automation, and the record of bids

Auctions depend on a third-party messaging network and on your device and connectivity. Messages can be delayed, reordered, undelivered, or lost, and an auction can close while a message of yours is in flight. That risk is yours. We do not guarantee delivery, timing, or availability, and we are not liable for a bid you were unable to place, or that arrived after a lot closed.

The ArtBound agent is automated and, like any such system, may misread a message or respond imperfectly. Nothing the agent says is a warranty about a lot; statements about lots come from the gallery. A bid is effective only where it appears as a confirmed bid in our records, and the gallery’s acceptance is what completes a sale.

Our server-side record of registrations, bids, confirmations, and timestamps is the authoritative record of what happened in a sale, and is conclusive in the absence of manifest error. Screenshots and local message histories are not. Raise any dispute over a bid with us within 48 hours of the close of the lot.

02What we collect

2.1 Identity and contact

Your name, WhatsApp number, WhatsApp profile name, email address, postal and billing address, country, and — where you bid as agent — the same for your principal.

2.2 Messages

The content of your auction thread: everything you send us and everything the agent sends you, including any images, documents, voice notes, or attachments you choose to send, together with timestamps and delivery status.

2.3 Auction and transaction data

Invitations and RSVPs, registration and paddle records, lots viewed, questions asked, bids confirmed and their exact times, outbid and hammer events, purchases, invoices, and payment status.

2.4 Verification data

Where identity or source-of-funds checks apply (see Terms 07): identity documents, date of birth, nationality, proof of address, beneficial-ownership details, and the results of sanctions and PEP screening.

2.5 Technical data

Server logs from the platform: IP address, request times, error and security events. If you visit artbound.art, your browser type and IP are visible to our hosting provider in the ordinary course of serving the page.

2.6 What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as CCPA/CPRA defines that term.
  • We do not run advertising trackers, browser fingerprinting, session replay, or behavioural profiling.
  • We do not use your messages, bids, or documents to train foundation or general-purpose AI models, and we require the same of our AI vendors.
  • We do not disclose your participation or your bids to other participants.

03WhatsApp and Meta

Auctions run over the WhatsApp Business Platform, operated by Meta. That means:

  • Meta processes your messages and the metadata around them (your number, timings, delivery receipts) under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy, which is outside our control.
  • WhatsApp encrypts messages in transit. That protects them from third parties — it does not hide them from us: we and the gallery can read what you send to the auction number, because we are the party you are messaging.
  • Your device holds its own copy of the thread and may back it up to your own cloud account. That copy is yours to manage.

If you would rather not use WhatsApp, tell the gallery — some sales can be run by email or telephone instead, at the gallery’s discretion.

04Automated processing and AI

The ArtBound agent is an AI system. Your messages are processed by large language models, supplied by third-party vendors acting as our processors under contract, in order to understand what you are asking and to reply — including to walk you through bidding, answer questions about a lot from the gallery’s material, and decline questions it should not answer.

Bidding logic itself is rule-based: increments, the confirmation step, the self-outbid block, anti-sniping, and reserve handling are deterministic, not model judgment. No decision producing a legal or similarly significant effect on you is made by AI alone — registration, verification outcomes, refusal of a bid, and the hammer are decided by us or the gallery, with human oversight. You may ask for human review of any such decision by writing to privacy@ntelio.ai.
